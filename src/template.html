A structured map of quantum computing and related quantum technologies. Search real bibliographic records and official learning resources, trace their metadata, and export the results you need.

Search and filter

Filters combine with AND. Fine tags are assigned only when supported by title wording; “topic level only” means a finer tag was not established. Saving is local to this browser.

Detailed subject taxonomy

Each major category contains topics and a finer vocabulary. Click a topic to filter the catalog. Click a fine subcategory to search that phrase within its topic. Categories overlap, and this map is broad rather than a claim that the field has a final exhaustive taxonomy.

Reading paths and research practice

Use these as suggested sequences through the catalog. They are not claims that every resource in a branch has the same difficulty or demonstrates practical quantum advantage.

Questions to take to every paper

  1. What exact task, input access model, observable, and accuracy are specified?
  2. Is the result a theorem, simulation, hardware experiment, review, tutorial, or roadmap?
  3. Does the cost include state preparation, oracles, compilation, shots, correction, and classical processing?
  4. What is the strongest appropriate classical baseline?
  5. Which noise model, device assumptions, and confidence intervals support the conclusion?
  6. Are code, circuit definitions, datasets, parameters, and environments sufficient to reproduce it?
